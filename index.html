Questions

Before you book.

Do you provide a driver?

No. DangGo Rides is self-drive only. You drive the car yourself, so you need a valid driving licence.

Can I pick the car up at the airport?

Yes. We offer pickup and drop at Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun. Send us your flight time on WhatsApp and we will plan around it.

What documents do I need?

A valid driving licence and a government photo ID. We tell you if anything else is needed when you confirm the booking.

How do I get the rates?

Rates depend on the car and the number of days, so we share them directly. Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 63965 60525.

Can I take the car to the hills?

Yes, our cars are for Uttarakhand roads. Tell us where you plan to go so we can share route tips and any limits for that trip.
